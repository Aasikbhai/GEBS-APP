GEBS MANAGMENT & EXAM  -  connecting PC 1, PC 2 and the School App

1) Build the installer:  double-click BUILD-SETUP-EXE.bat  (allow Administrator).  Installer = release\GEBS MANAGMENT & EXAM Setup.exe
2) In the software: Settings > "PC 1 <-> PC 2 Data Transfer" shows this PC's address (example 192.168.1.20).
3) Add students (Full Name + Registration No.) and teachers (Full Name + Registration No.) in the software.
   The Registration No. is the School App password.  (Teacher: if Registration No. is empty, the Employee ID is used.)
4) School App: type the PC address on the login screen, then sign in with Full Name + Registration No.
5) Keep the software open on the PC. Windows Firewall: click "Allow access" (Private network) the first time.
